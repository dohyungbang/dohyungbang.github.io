I am a Ph.D. candidate in the [White Lodging-J.W. Marriott, Jr. School of Hospitality and Tourism Management](https://hhs.purdue.edu/htm/) at Purdue University. Prior to joining Purdue, I earned a bachelor's degree in Tourism Management and a master's degree in Hospitality Management from Kyung Hee University, South Korea.

My specific research interests include:
- Tourism Economics
- Hospitality Business Analytics
- Computational Social Science
- Dynamics in Home Sharing Economy

In addition to my personal research, I work as a research assistant at the [Center for Hospitality and Retail Industries Business Analytics (CHRIBA)](https://www.chriba-purdue.com/) at Purdue, where I contribute to producing industry reports aimed at supporting stakeholders in these sectors.
