I am an Assistant Professor of Hospitality Business Analytics at [Conrad N. Hilton College of Global Hospitality Leadership, University of Houston](https://uh.edu/hilton-college) since August 2026. 

My specific research interests include:
- Hospitality Business Analytics
- Applied Economics in Hospitality and Tourism
- Efficiency and Productivity Analysis
- Sharing Economy

Specifically, I examine **micro-level dynamics within the hospitality and experience economy ecosystem**, while also exploring **how complex tourism systems at the macro level shape societal and economic outcomes**. My work employs various analytical methods and computational/statistical techniques to analyze large-scale, high-resolution datasets (e.g., user-generated content, mobility data, consumer spending, and point-of-interest data). Through this research, my ultimate goal is to provide credible, data-driven evidence that advances understanding of the complex and evolving dynamics of hospitality and tourism.
